Open HCI Project - 抒花 Blossom

Project Introduction

I attended the Open HCI program and design “Blossom” with my teammate. We earned the first prize. Thus, we attend TAICHI 2022 and CSCW 2022 in Demo session.

Background

During the Covid-19 pandemic, working from home(WFH) has become the main way to work. However, this change resulted in blurry boundaries between work and rest, increasing the work stress of people, and letting people become more tired.
43.4% of workers considered the working hours of WFH is blurry.
38.4% of workers consider on-call lead to emotional tension and increase psychological stress
75.8% of workers still choose WFH

Goal

How might we remind home workers to be aware of their own work stress, let them take a rest, stay healthy and happy?
